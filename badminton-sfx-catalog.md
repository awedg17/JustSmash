## Implementation Notes for Just Smash

### Pitch Variation Strategy
Racket hit sounds should vary by shot type:
- **Smash:** Lower pitch, louder, more bass (crack/thwack)
- **Clear:** Mid-high pitch, clean ring
- **Drop:** Soft, high-pitched tap, minimal resonance
- **Drive:** Flat, sharp, short duration

### Layering Approach
Each rally moment layers multiple SFX simultaneously:
1. Base layer: `crowd_ambient` (always playing, ducked during hits)
2. Action layer: `racket_hit_*` + `shoe_squeak` + `footwork_steps`
3. Impact layer: `shuttle_floor_impact` or `net_cord`
4. Reaction layer: `crowd_cheer_*` or `crowd_applause` (triggered on point end)
5. Vocal layer: `player_grunt` (on smash) or `player_shout_celebrate` (on point win)

### Doubles-Specific Sounds
- `racket_clash_doubles` — rare but adds authenticity
- Partner communication shouts
- Interception-specific quick-hit sounds

### Crowd Dynamics
- Ramp `crowd_rally_buildup` intensity based on rally length (>10 shots = louder)
- `crowd_rhythmic_clap` ("ea ea") triggerable by home-team momentum streaks
- Duck ambient crowd during umpire calls
- Peak volume on match point
